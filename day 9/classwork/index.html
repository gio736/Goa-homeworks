my first favourite dish is katleti

Quick Georgian Cutlets (Kotleti) Ingredients: 500g Mixed ground meat (Beef/Pork) 1 Onion & 2 cloves Garlic (minced) 2 slices Bread (soaked in water/milk) Handful of Cilantro/Parsley (chopped) Salt, pepper, and oil for frying Flour or breadcrumbs (for coating) Instructions: Mix: Combine meat, squeezed bread, onion, garlic, herbs, and spices. Add a splash of cold water and knead well. Shape: Form oval patties and coat lightly in flour or breadcrumbs. Fry: Cook in a hot oiled pan for 5 minutes per side until golden brown. Finish: Cover with a lid and steam on low heat for 5 more minutes.

my second favourite dish is kebab

Quick Georgian Kebab Ingredients: 500g Ground meat (Beef/Pork or Beef/Lamb mix) 1 Onion (finely minced) 2 cloves Garlic (minced) 1 tsp Dried coriander & 1 tsp Blue fenugreek (Utskho Suneli) To taste: Salt, black pepper, and chili flakes For serving: Red onion slices, sumac, and Lavash bread Instructions: Mix: Combine meat, onion, garlic, and all spices. Knead very well for 5–10 minutes until the meat becomes sticky. Shape: Mold the meat around skewers (or shape into long, thin logs if using a pan). Cook: Grill over charcoal or fry in a hot pan for 8–10 minutes, turning often until browned and juicy. Serve: Wrap in warm Lavash, top with sliced onions mixed with sumac, and enjoy!

my third favourite dish is piure

აი, კლასიკური, ჰაეროვანი კარტოფილის პიურეს (Mashed Potatoes) სწრაფი რეცეპტი ინგლისურად: Creamy Mashed Potatoes Ingredients: 1 kg Potatoes (peeled and cubed) 50g Butter 1/2 cup Warm milk or heavy cream To taste: Salt and black pepper Instructions: Boil: Place potatoes in salted water. Bring to a boil and cook for 15-20 minutes until fork-tender. Drain the water. Mash: Mash the hot potatoes thoroughly until no lumps remain. Mix: Add the butter and warm milk. Stir vigorously until smooth, creamy, and fluffy. Season: Add salt and pepper to taste, and serve hot!

my fourth favourite dish is khinkali

Quick Homemade Khinkali Ingredients: For the Dough: 500g flour, 1 tsp salt, ~200ml water (dough must be very firm). For the Filling: 500g ground meat (beef/pork mix), 1 onion (finely minced), salt, black pepper, chili flakes, and 1 cup of cold water (kneaded into the meat for the soup). Instructions: Make Dough: Mix flour, salt, and water. Knead into a hard dough, let it rest for 20 minutes, then roll out thin. Cut into circles. Make Filling: Mix the meat with onion and spices, then slowly add cold water until the meat absorbs it. Fold: Place a spoonful of meat in the center of a dough circle. Pleat the edges together to form a pouch, twisting the top into a "knot" (kudi). Boil: Drop into boiling salted water. Stir gently with a wooden spoon so they don't stick. Cook for 7–9 minutes after they float to the top.

my favourite fifth dish is chees pasta

Quick Cheese Pasta Ingredients: 250g Pasta (Penne, Macaroni, or Spaghetti) 1 cup Grated cheese (Cheddar, Gouda, or Sulguni) 2 tbsp Butter 1/2 cup Milk or heavy cream To taste: Salt, black pepper, and garlic powder Instructions: Boil: Cook pasta in boiling salted water according to package instructions. Drain, but reserve 1/4 cup of pasta water. Make Sauce: In the warm pot, melt butter over low heat. Add milk (or cream) and bring to a gentle simmer. Melt Cheese: Turn off the heat. Stir in the grated cheese until completely melted and smooth. (If too thick, add a splash of the reserved pasta water). Combine: Toss the cooked pasta into the cheese sauce. Season with black pepper and garlic powder.
