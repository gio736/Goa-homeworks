1. Khinkali (Georgian Dumplings) Ingredients Dough: 500g flour, 1 tsp salt, ~250ml water. Filling: 500g minced meat (beef/pork mix), 1 onion (finely chopped), 100ml warm water or broth, cilantro, salt, and black pepper. Instructions Make the dough: Mix flour, salt, and water. Knead into a very firm, smooth dough. Let it rest for 30 minutes. Make the filling: Mix the meat, onion, spices, and herbs. Gradually add warm water to the meat so it becomes juicy (this creates the broth inside). Assemble: Roll out the dough and cut into circles. Roll each circle thin. Put a tablespoon of meat in the center, pleat the edges together to seal it, and twist the top to form a knob. Cook: Drop into boiling salted water. Stir gently so they don't stick. Cook for 7–10 minutes after they float up upside down. Serve with black pepper.



2. Kababi (Minced Meat Kebabs) Ingredients 500g minced meat (beef, pork, or lamb mix). 1 onion and 2 cloves of garlic (finely minced). 1 tsp dried coriander, 1 tsp blue fenugreek (utskho suneli), red pepper flakes, salt, and black pepper. To serve: Lavashi (flatbread), sumac, and sliced red onions. Instructions Mix: Combine the meat, onion, garlic, and all the spices. Knead the mixture thoroughly with your hands for about 5–10 minutes until it becomes sticky and holds together. Shape: Mold the meat mixture around long wooden or metal skewers into elongated sausage shapes. Cook: Grill over charcoal or fry in a hot pan with a little oil for 10–12 minutes, turning frequently until browned and cooked through. Serve: Wrap the hot kebab in lavashi bread and top with sliced onions and sumac.



3. Adjarian Khachapuri (Boat-Shaped Cheese Bread) Ingredients Dough: 400g flour, 1 tsp yeast, 1 tsp sugar, 1 tsp salt, 250ml warm water/milk, 2 tbsp oil. Filling: 400g cheese (Sulguni and Imeretian mix, or Mozzarella + Feta), 1 tbsp butter, 1 egg yolk per boat. Instructions Make the dough: Mix the ingredients, knead into a soft dough, and let it rise in a warm place for 1 hour. Shape: Divide the dough. Roll into an oval, roll the two long edges inward, and pinch the ends together to create a boat shape. Fill and Bake: Fill the center generously with shredded cheese. Bake in a preheated oven at 220°C (430°F) for about 12–15 minutes until the crust is golden. The Final Touch: Take it out, drop a raw egg yolk and a slice of butter in the center. Serve immediately—mix the cheese, butter, and egg together using pieces of the crust!
